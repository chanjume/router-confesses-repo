# The Router Confesses

[![make.fun bounty](https://img.shields.io/badge/make.fun-bounty-blue)](https://make.fun)
[![entry](https://img.shields.io/badge/entry-one--line-green)](https://make.fun)
[![license](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-lightgrey)](LICENSE)

One line, 113 characters: a home Wi-Fi router excuses itself for dying
at the climax of a movie — right as the killer unmasked himself, at
11:47, after years of service as NETGEAR-5G without a single thank you.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

> I cut out at 11:47, right as the killer unmasked himself — you named me NETGEAR-5G and never once said thank you.
